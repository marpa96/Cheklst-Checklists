## Fuel budget

```calc
title Trip fuel cost
input distance = 300 | Distance (mi)
input mpg = 30 | Fuel economy (mpg)
input price = 3.50 | Gas price ($/gal)
let gallons = distance / mpg
show stops = ceil(gallons / 12) | Fill-ups (12 gal tank)
show cost = round(gallons * price, 2) | Fuel cost ($)
```

- [ ] Budget ${{cost}} for fuel
